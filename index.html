How to Play

  1. Shout into your mic — the louder you meow, the harder you push the clash meter toward the enemy.
  2. When the meter reaches the enemy's side, you land a hit and deal damage.
  3. When it reaches your side, you take a hit — so keep pushing!
  4. Drain the enemy's HP to 0 to win the level and earn coins.
  5. Press F when the MEGA MEOW bar is full for a massive attack.
  6. Spend coins in the Skin Shop for new looks and voices.

Tip: use keyboard mode (SPACE to roar) if you don't have a mic.
